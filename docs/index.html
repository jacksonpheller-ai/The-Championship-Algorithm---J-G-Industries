How the desk works

Every day the engine pulls the slate, injury report, DraftKings lines and player props, rebuilds every team and player model from games already played, simulates each game 20,000 times and sizes bets on the joint outcomes. It runs on GitHub's servers at about 10:40 am and 5:15 pm Eastern, grades the previous night at about 4:20 am, and refits itself every Monday.

Data points

  • Team efficiency: offensive and defensive rating per 100 possessions from every box score this season, adjusted for opponent and home court by weighted ridge regression with a 45-day half-life. Pace modelled the same way.
  • Four factors (eFG%, turnover rate, offensive rebound rate, free-throw rate, both ends), SRS, strength of schedule and true shooting from Basketball-Reference.
  • Player value: Box Plus/Minus split into offensive and defensive BPM, blended with each player's individual Offensive Rating (points produced per 100 possessions, weighted by usage) and individual Defensive Rating (points allowed per 100 while on court). This season and last are blended by minutes; PER, TS%, usage, WS/48 and VORP are shown on every rotation.
  • Roster-talent prior: each team starts the season from last year's ratings regressed to the mean, blended with the minutes-weighted value of the players it is actually using now.
  • Injuries: ESPN statuses become play probabilities (Out 0%, Doubtful 15%, Questionable 50%, Probable 90%); the change in expected minutes times each player's value above replacement moves the team's ratings.
  • Schedule: rest days, back-to-backs, three games in four nights, travel miles, time-zone shifts and altitude. Coefficients fit from four seasons of results.
  • Elo: a margin-of-victory Elo rating as an independent second opinion.
  • Market: moneyline, spread and total with prices, de-vigged (power method for moneylines), plus opening lines.
  • Props: per-minute rates (this season and last), projected minutes, vacated minutes and usage from absent teammates, the opponent's allowed rate for that stat, pace and blowout risk, drawn from negative binomial distributions inside the game simulation.

From projection to price

The model's margin and total are blended with the market line. The weights were fit out of sample and are deliberately small: 10% model on spreads and 30% on totals against a morning line, less near tip-off, because closing lines are hard to beat. Outcomes are simulated from the empirical distribution of past errors rather than a bell curve. Prop probabilities come from a logistic model fit on real pre-game prop results.

Parlays

Parlays only combine legs from different games, so their odds of hitting are the product of the legs' probabilities, computed exactly rather than read off the simulation (reading them off the simulation systematically flatters the combos that got lucky in the draws). Books pay parlays at the product of the leg prices, so a parlay of positive-EV legs compounds the edge, at the cost of hitting rarely. Same-game parlays are not recommended because books price their correlation.

Sizing, including a $10 bankroll

Bets need at least 1.5% expected value and an edge under 12 points of probability. Stakes come from a Kelly optimiser over all candidates at once, using the joint simulation so bets on the same game are sized down together. A fractional-Kelly profile bets as if the bankroll were that fraction of what you entered. Stakes are whole units of your smallest bet: with $10 and $0.50 units each unit is 5% of the bankroll, so the optimiser puts the money into one to four positions, often a parlay, and skips bets whose edge cannot carry a 5% position. If nothing qualifies at your profile, that is the answer for the night.

House rules

  • Bet the number shown or better. If the line has moved past it, skip the bet.
  • Lines come from one book. Shopping the same bet across books is the cheapest edge there is.
  • Late scratches change everything. The desk refreshes before tip; check the injury report.
  • Only bet money you can afford to lose. Betting is legal only where licensed and for adults of legal age. Help: 1-800-GAMBLER.
